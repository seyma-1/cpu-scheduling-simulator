Scheduling rules

FCFS

Runs ready processes in arrival order. A running process is not interrupted.

SJF

When the CPU becomes free, chooses the ready process with the shortest original burst. Non-preemptive.

SRTF

Chooses the shortest remaining time. The running process is preempted only by a strictly shorter remaining time.

Round Robin

Uses a FIFO queue. Arrivals at a quantum boundary join the queue before the unfinished process is re-enqueued.
